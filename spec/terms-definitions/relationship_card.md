[[def: r-card, relationship card, r-cards, relationship cards]]

~ A [[ref: VDS]] containing a combination of human-readable and machine-readable data describing the publisher. R-cards are typically exchanged in conjunction with [verifiable relationship credentials](https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-cred-spec/blob/main/spec/terms-definitions/verifiable_relationship_credential.md) (VRCs). As a VDS, an r-card may be encapsulated in a standard [[ref: VC]]. One use of r-cards is to serve as a modern, self-updating version of a [vCard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VCard).

~ Source: [DTG Credentials specification](https://github.com/trustoverip/dtgwg-cred-spec/blob/main/spec/terms-definitions/relationship_card.md).
